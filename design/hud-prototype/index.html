Prototype

The simulated song plays at its own tempo; while you compare, it loops the bars being compared. Hover the meter for a readout; click or drag on it to see the tracks for those bars. Click the speed (1×) or scroll over the meter to make it move faster or slower. Red marks under the meter are dropouts, the dashed line is the peak of the last 3 seconds, and the meter edge glows while the level stays over the alert level (Settings). ☰ opens the side menu, the pin keeps the app above your DAW (here it only shows the setting), the magnifier opens search, ▾ shows or hides the tracks. System and Time are in the side menu; their Prototype buttons fake an unplugged interface, a sample-rate mismatch, or time passing. All values are generated in this page; nothing is a measurement and no DAW integration exists yet.
